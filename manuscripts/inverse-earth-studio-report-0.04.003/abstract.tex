Geophysical inversion is underdetermined: observations constrain a subsurface model through acquisition geometry, a forward operator, error assumptions, parameterization, prior information, and numerical optimization. Inverse Earth Studio connects data ingestion, processing, forward modelling, inversion, diagnostics, and interpretation across potential fields, electromagnetics, electrical methods, and seismology. It includes Python pipelines, solvers, didactic notebooks, provenance-bearing experiments, browser instruments, and a bounded authenticated service.

The implemented methods cover gravity and magnetic processing and inversion, magnetotelluric screening and layered-earth inversion, electrical-resistivity quality control and inversion, waveform processing and phase picking, travel-time inversion, acoustic full-waveform inversion, joint inversion, and learned estimators. Results include unresolved cases: a synthetic gravity fit has weighted WRMS 1 but model correlation 0.283 and centroid depth about 570 m for a 282 m truth; a low-velocity FWI case remains unresolved; and Clear Lake station cl061 fails the 1D consistency screen. The geometry-normalized travel-time study is negative on its synthetic joint-shift cohort: two same-seed CUDA runs have learned-to-reference velocity RMSE ratios 2.194 and 2.190, and travel-time ratios 5.585 and 5.595. Every case exceeds the validation threshold; this is neither field-performance nor independent-training-seed evidence.

A processed 2002--03 BAS SPARC survey adds measured-data M03 checks. MagL flight/tie intersections have RMSE 36.780 nT; paired blocked-profile interpolation has RMSE 223.900 nT for MagL and 223.979 nT for MagML. The sensor elevations differ at crossings, so these are descriptive QC and interpolation results, not leveling error or inversion evidence. The workbench distinguishes data fit, model recovery, and geological interpretation; known truth belongs to synthetic cases, while field observations lack it. Replayed artifacts and browser calculations are identified separately. The report documents methods, numerical assumptions, provenance, executable results, and limitations without claiming unresolved scientific or service gates have passed. Null-space projection is established prior methodology, not a novel product contribution.
