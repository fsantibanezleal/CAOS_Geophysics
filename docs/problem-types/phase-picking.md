# Earthquake P/S phase picking: M08 and M13 design record

Status: approved method design and explanatory UI content only. This repository has not yet established an M08 field-pick result, trained M13 checkpoint, held-out event/station benchmark or browser inference parity. These methods operate on earthquake station waveforms, not on the active-source shot gathers used for acoustic FWI.

## Data and physical units

The future input chain is a rights-reviewed MiniSEED waveform, matching StationXML channel response, event/station identity and independently sourced analyst phase picks. MiniSEED digital samples are counts. A response-corrected derivative may be ground velocity in m/s only when channel, epoch, stabilization and output-unit choices are documented. The original bytes remain immutable: ObsPy response removal changes an in-memory stream in place. Each selected window records UTC start, sample rate `f_s` in Hz, three-channel order/orientation, gaps, clipped samples, response epoch, detrend/taper/filter/resampling operations and hashes of the original and derivative. The sample timestamp is `t_n=t_0+n/f_s`; `n` is dimensionless and `t_n` is UTC time. A count trace must not be labelled m/s. [ObsPy response lookup](https://docs.obspy.org/packages/autogen/obspy.core.inventory.inventory.Inventory.get_response.html), [response removal](https://docs.obspy.org/packages/autogen/obspy.core.stream.Stream.remove_response.html), [FDSN StationXML](https://docs.fdsn.org/projects/stationxml/en/latest/).

## M08: classical onset and phase association

For a consistently labelled channel `x[n]`, a classical characteristic compares short and long squared-amplitude averages. `N_S=round(f_s T_S)` and `N_L=round(f_s T_L)` convert window durations in seconds to sample counts, with `N_L>N_S`. The ratio `C[n]=mean_short(x^2)/mean_long(x^2)` is dimensionless whether `x` is counts or m/s, but invalid denominators, gaps and incomplete long windows are not valid detections. Trigger-on/off thresholds propose intervals, not phase labels. P and S association requires a separate, documented phase window and component/polarization evidence; held-out analyst labels must not choose the prediction. An S candidate can be confused with P coda or a converted phase; choosing the second trigger is not a physical classifier. A published S workflow adds polarization, AR-AIC refinement and quality checks, but these are not frozen M08 settings. See [Allen's 1978 onset picker](https://doi.org/10.1785/bssa0680051521), [ObsPy classic STA/LTA](https://docs.obspy.org/packages/autogen/obspy.signal.trigger.classic_sta_lta.html), [ObsPy trigger source](https://docs.obspy.org/_modules/obspy/signal/trigger.html), and [Diehl et al.'s S-wave picker](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120080019) with its [reproducibility supplement](https://www.seismosoc.org/Publications/BSSA_html/bssa_99-3/2008019-esupp/).

The method-specific implementation gate will freeze filter corners, short/long windows, on/off thresholds, phase-association rule, duplicate/refractory handling and abstention on training/validation groups. None of those values is frozen by this content change. A result must retain candidate onset, UTC P/S pick or unresolved reason, channel evidence, ratio/threshold, preprocessing and source hashes. Comparison to an analyst pick yields signed `delta_t=t_pick-t_analyst` in seconds, not a comparison to an exact physical arrival.

## M13: PhaseNet-family learned picking

[Zhu and Beroza's PhaseNet paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggy423) describes three-component, per-component mean/std normalized input; its reference data were sampled at 100 Hz and its illustrated window has 3001 samples, or 30.0 s between endpoints. The network adapts a 1D U-Net with four downsampling and four upsampling stages and skip connections. At each sample, softmax scores for background, P and S sum to one. Analyst picks become Gaussian-smoothed targets with a 0.1 s reference width, and categorical cross-entropy sums over classes and time. The [official AI4EPS code](https://github.com/AI4EPS/PhaseNet) is a candidate implementation. These architecture and label values are from the paper, not a product checkpoint or demonstrated local hyperparameters.

Normalization turns a consistently labelled count or m/s trace into a dimensionless tensor. The selected product training run must keep channel order, physical input representation, anti-alias resampling, windowing, zero-variance/missing-channel policy and train-only augmentation identical at inference. A score peak requires fixed phase thresholds, peak spacing and quality checks. The score is not automatically calibrated pick uncertainty. Analyst disagreement, time-base error, out-of-distribution instruments, low signal-to-noise ratio, clipping, gaps and overlapping events must be evaluated separately.

## Disjoint same-trace evaluation and release boundary

The approved Geophysics SDD demands a stricter test than the PhaseNet paper's station-stratified recording split: training, validation and test must have no event ID overlap **and** no station identity overlap. Assign both identities before fitting transforms or selecting thresholds. A bridge recording whose event and station belong to different partitions is excluded and counted. If this makes a meaningful test infeasible, record not-run and revisit data acquisition; do not silently relax the rule. This is a proposed product protocol, not a published PhaseNet result.

M08 and M13 receive identical eligible held-out trace IDs, UTC windows, QC flags and independent analyst picks. Their declared preprocessing may differ, but the comparison population and exclusion ledger cannot. One-to-one phase matching uses predeclared P and S timing tolerances. Report per-phase precision, recall, false picks, misses, P/S swaps, signed and absolute timing residuals, abstentions, signal-to-noise and instrument strata. Assess score calibration separately from conditional timing error. Do not select a visually successful subset or quote paper metrics as local evidence.

Acceptance still requires rights-cleared real traces and checkpoint terms, pinned numerical execution, event/station-disjoint M08-versus-M13 results with negative controls, and an exported browser model that matches canonical inference on the same arrays within a measured numerical, latency and memory budget. Until those gates pass, the public Methodology and Implementation sections are educational design, not an available online method.
