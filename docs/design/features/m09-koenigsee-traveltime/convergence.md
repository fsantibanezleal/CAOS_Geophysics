The earlier claim that one engine thread had closed reproducibility is withdrawn. A later independent rerun of the unchanged branch failed three M09 tests at the immutable receipt comparison. The old ignored `koenigsee-m09-inverse-821d1889ddbb.json` receipt remains byte-for-byte intact but is **superseded as repeatability evidence**. Fixed-model field forward times, starting gradient and fixed-model Jacobian agreed across fresh processes, while the first nonlinear inversion step diverged with the default adaptive CGLS inner solve. This locates the observed instability in the inner linearized inversion update; it does not identify a unique low-level floating-point cause. Single-thread and external BLAS/OpenMP thread settings alone were insufficient. Explicit tighter inner CGLS settings produced matching predicted-time digests in two isolated primary-fit probes, and the final full runs passed the stronger receipt comparison without relaxing the original whole-shot or stopping gates. The new ignored `koenigsee-m09-inverse-c101cda07a5f.json` is the final-code local receipt.
