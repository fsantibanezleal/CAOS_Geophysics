# M09 Koenigsee first-arrival traveltime requirements

Date: 2026-09-28. Parent: [approved product SDD](../../SDD.md), M09 and sections 2, 4-6. Scope: an offline, provider-linked field-pick vertical. No API, frontend, host, canonical bake or public field artifact is in scope. The pinned `koenigsee.sgt` is 9,844 bytes (SHA-256 `cf8f6c8c79fd0f60984eefc61aff67b567b0c875f8f5c663fe904cdbf0d0414a`) with 63 survey points and 714 picks. The verified local file has 15 shot groups, 48 receiver points, no reciprocal pair, and no supplied error column. Its header names `x`, `y`, `s`, `g`, `t` but not units or a CRS. The official pyGIMLi example and manager convention support a *declared provider interpretation* of metres and seconds; they do not make those units file-declared. The fixed upstream example-data tree has no licence file or rights statement granting field-data redistribution.

| ID | EARS requirement | Named gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TT-01 | WHEN Koenigsee is selected, THE local workflow SHALL reverify the ledger-pinned byte count, SHA-256 and immutable acquisition receipt, retain the original in ignored storage, and refuse public raw or field-result output. | `tests/data/test_traveltime_data.py::test_pinned_source_and_ignored_receipt` and `git ls-files data/downloads data/raw` |
| TT-02 | WHEN an `.sgt` is parsed, THE source-specific adapter SHALL require exact count markers and `#x y`/`#s g t` columns, finite strictly increasing survey x and finite y, 1-based in-range distinct shot/geophone indices, unique pairs, finite positive seconds, and no undeclared trailer. It SHALL fail with a field or row reason rather than silently repairing malformed data. | `tests/data/test_traveltime_data.py::test_strict_sgt_contract` |
| TT-03 | WHEN QC runs, THE workflow SHALL preserve every accepted pick, report shot/receiver counts and spatial coverage, source-receiver offsets, time and apparent-speed ranges, duplicates, reciprocal availability and flag-only gross/ordering anomalies. IF the source has no measured error or reciprocal pair, THEN THE workflow SHALL not report calibrated precision. | `tests/data/test_traveltime_data.py::test_qc_preserves_picks_and_absent_errors` |
| TT-04 | WHEN a forward engine is used, THE workflow SHALL check a separately computed homogeneous straight-line `t=distance/v` oracle against pyGIMLi Dijkstra times at the fixed test geometry, with maximum relative error at most `1e-3`, and verify reciprocal/speed scaling there. The field inverse SHALL retain surveyed x/y topography. | `tests/numerics/test_traveltime.py::test_homogeneous_forward_oracle` and `::test_homogeneous_time_and_field_holdout` |
| TT-05 | WHILE field pick errors are absent, WHEN inversion is attempted, THE workflow SHALL label `sigma_t=max(0.03*t, 0.0001 s)` as an assumed weighting rule, use a pinned pyGIMLi engine with fixed mesh/gradient/regularization/stopping settings, and record source, code, environment, configuration and canonical physical mesh fingerprints in an ignored checksummed receipt. | `tests/numerics/test_traveltime.py::test_field_receipt_and_uncertainty_boundary` |
| TT-06 | WHEN a field inverse is evaluated, THE workflow SHALL fit without three predeclared whole-shot groups (every fifth shot in x order) and independently without the three central shot groups in a second fit. Each split SHALL compare held-out absolute-time RMSE to its own training-only weighted homogeneous-slowness baseline, retain per-shot and signed per-pick residuals, and report raypath-length coverage, start-model and mesh sensitivity. A predictive inverse SHALL require finite positive model/forward times, normal engine stop before 20 iterations, at least 10% aggregate held-out RMSE improvement on **both** splits, and improvement for at least two of three held shots on each split. | `tests/numerics/test_traveltime.py::test_homogeneous_time_and_field_holdout` and `::test_whole_shot_splits_and_failure_gate` |
| TT-07 | IF the independent forward check, source suitability, solver stop or either held-out gate fails, THEN THE workflow SHALL retain source QC and attempted metrics locally but return `unverified`, `ineligible` or `not-converged`, never a successful field velocity interpretation. | `tests/numerics/test_traveltime.py::test_whole_shot_splits_and_failure_gate` |
| TT-08 | THE feature convergence and source/theory guides SHALL document provenance, unresolved rights, provider-based units, eikonal/slowness physics, local reproduction, error assumptions, residual/coverage meaning, group leakage limits and all unmet gates. | `tests/data/test_traveltime_data.py::test_m09_documented_boundaries` and `docs/design/features/m09-koenigsee-traveltime/convergence.md` |
| TT-09 | WHEN a field inverse is reported as predictive, THE workflow SHALL pin the inner CGLS linear solve as well as the outer inversion, verify the same source, cell order and forward/model outputs on independent fresh-process fits, and reject a prediction drift above 0.01 ms or model drift above `0.1 m/s + 1e-4` relative. It SHALL preserve prior receipts without overwriting them and retain the original held-shot/stop gates. | `tests/numerics/test_traveltime.py::test_independent_process_fit_reproducibility`, `::test_field_receipt_and_uncertainty_boundary`, and repeated full local CLI runs |

The whole-shot partitions and thresholds are declared before field inversion code or results. The analytic oracle validates homogeneous sign, scale and geometry, not heterogeneous geology. A held-out shot still shares receivers and one survey with training, so success is within-profile prediction only. No field model is geological truth, a calibrated posterior, an independent-site validation or a public case without a separate rights decision.
