# CPU2 terminal fixed-point certificate amendment

**Status: owner-approved for design, implementation, and validation on 2026-10-31.**
This additive amendment applies only to the corrected CPU2 policy. It does not
change CPU1, the original objective, candidate grid, observation splits,
epsilon schedule, per-fit or aggregate limits, or the 20 accepted weight-update
cap.

## Purpose and exact scope

The existing terminal accepts only when the last three model and weight
transitions are each at most `1e-6`. At update 20 and the epsilon floor, that
history rule can reject a state that is itself an exact fixed point of the
prescribed IRLS map. The new certificate is evaluated only after all 20
updates, when the existing terminal rule fails. The existing path remains
first and unchanged; all other failures remain failures.

The certificate is accepted only if every condition below holds:

1. The exact CPU2 source, request, bounds, beta, stage, epsilon floor, stage
   operator and all 21 stage records pass the ordinary replay.
2. This is the non-null `interior_unique_nonzero_max` branch, with no active
   bound coordinate and no tied maximum.
3. The final native inner solve reports `absolute_stationary`, has zero
   iterations, has no failed trial, and its accepted start and stop `q` arrays
   are bitwise identical.
4. The final stage's recomputed smallness weights are bitwise identical to the
   exact weights used to define the frozen quadratic.
5. The independently recomputed projected KKT infinity norm is at most
   `1e-12`.
6. The smallness component's fixed Hessian is diagonal with finite, strictly
   positive entries. Its computed lower bound
   `beta_engine * min(diag(H_smallness))` is finite and positive. The data
   Hessian and the remaining fixed quadratic regularization Hessians are
   positive semidefinite, so this bound proves the complete frozen Hessian is
   positive definite. The projected KKT point is therefore the unique global
   minimizer on the unchanged box.
7. The recorded certificate proof values and source/objective identities
   reconstruct exactly during replay. No update, optimizer call, step, or
   tolerance relaxation is added.

The certificate directly establishes the IRLS fixed-point equations: the
current model is the exact stationary solution of the frozen quadratic, and
the weights that define that quadratic are exactly the weights generated by
that model. Strict convexity establishes uniqueness. It is not a claim of a
global minimizer for the nonquadratic L1 objective or of a field-valid model.

## Required controls

Positive controls must include every implicated archived CPU2 candidate 3–6
fold that meets the certificate conditions, candidate 6 fold 2 specifically,
an unchanged three-transition success, a null/reference result, and a distinct
seeded non-null result. The independent test oracle recomputes exact weights,
projected KKT and the positive smallness-Hessian lower bound from the original
request and the exact native state.

Adversarial controls must reject: any nonzero weight mismatch (including one
below `1e-6`); KKT above `1e-12`; a nonstationary or nonzero-iteration inner
solve; a failed solve; a nonpositive or nonfinite smallness lower bound;
altered source, request, objective, beta, epsilon, operator or bounds; a
one-ULP state mismatch; and a tied-maximum or active-bound branch. Existing
three-transition, null, resource, archive and replay controls remain required.

The evidence archive that motivated this amendment is diagnostic only. New
source-bound calibration archives are mandatory. All 24 family/condition
cells, noise/refit and resource controls, independent review, whole-product
gates and VPS release gates remain required before release.
