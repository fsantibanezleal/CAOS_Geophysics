## Physical scope and independent equations

For one supported corrected vertical channel in an exterior source-free region, the Cartesian harmonic interpolant is `f(r)=sum_j c_j / |r-s_j|`. The equivalent source coefficients have units mGal m and are **not** density, mass, source depth or an Earth interpretation. The source plane is explicitly `min(training receiver upward)-depth`; source XY are training stations only. Every source is below every applicable receiver. Fits and fold geometries never contain outer-heldout observations or source points.

Let `J` be the engine's inverse-distance Jacobian, `S=diag(column standard deviations)`, `A=J S^-1`, `W=diag(1/sigma_i^2)`. For positive damping `lambda`, `q=(A.T W A+lambda I)^-1 A.T W d`, `c=S^-1 q`. A target matrix `B=J_target S^-1` gives `p=L d`, `L=B(A.T W A+lambda I)^-1 A.T W`. Our diagnostic reconstruction must agree with the real engine prediction before any propagated error is accepted. For declared data covariance `C`, conditional propagated variance is `diag(L C L.T)`. This is sampling-error propagation conditional on fixed geometry, chosen depth/damping and parameter selection, not a posterior, bias estimate or geological confidence. A full supplied covariance can be propagated even though the official fit uses only diagonal marginal weights; it is not called GLS.

For an independent prism control: `g_down(r)=1e5 G integral_V rho (z-z')/|r-r'|^3 dV'`, `G=6.67430e-11` SI. Tensor-product Gauss-Legendre volume quadrature generates off-centre multi-prism fields; convergence across orders is checked before comparing with the analytic prism engine. This operator has a vertical derivative kernel and volumetric bodies, unlike the fitted scalar inverse-distance layer. At least three irregular/rotated/translated survey and offset-body geometries are required. It is not sufficient to fit the same equivalent sources that generated the observations.

## Selection, resolution and failure decisions

Freeze outer spatial blocks, seed, inner folds, candidate depths/dampings, heights, region/spacing, coverage radius, input-error ceiling and transfer-error ceiling before fitting. Select depth/damping by inner-training-block normalized RMSE, with explicit coverage fractions and a minimum count. Select the **lowest** candidate height meeting a predeclared training-only conditional transfer-SD ceiling over covered grid cells. Height changes physical target elevation: never score an elevated map against station values at a different elevation. Every candidate-height map, error bound, roughness and comparison remains exported. If no height meets the ceiling, return an explicit unmet-height-precision verdict, not an invented success or coarser replacement.

All map predictions are masked outside the training convex hull or beyond the training coverage radius. Excluded input masks are user-supplied, reversible and hash-preserved; outlier flags alone are not masks. Missing coordinates/errors fail even on masked rows because input geometry/provenance cannot be reconstructed otherwise. Grid sampling is not physical resolving power: reject extreme oversampling, preserve actual station spacing/coverage and report that continuation attenuates short wavelengths. Damped/unregularized operator condition numbers are separate; nearly singular candidates fail rather than display attractive unstable maps. A fitted layer is nonunique across depth/damping, and alternate fits/coefficient norms/maps remain visible.

Negative controls include wrong units/components, unresolved datum, missing coordinates or sigma, altered correction history, masks without reasons, oversized/nonfinite requests, collinear geometry, insufficient blocks, high declared noise exceeding the frozen precision budget and downward continuation. The attributed 2929-row author profile remains ineligible: no unknown datum, errors or original-processing lineage is synthesized.

## Geometry and correction integration seam

The existing correction contract stays untouched (`EPSG:4326`, WGS84 receiver heights, known replayable state). The transform receives that full correction result plus an explicitly cited, station-keyed metric Cartesian mapping and fixed-geometry conditional-error declaration. It validates correction replay/hash, propagated errors and exact receiver heights without producing another correction or changing originals. The mapping is supplied, never inferred from NAD27/`zWGS84` labels or fabricated metre coordinates. Bounded local Cartesian extent is at most 20 km; regional/spherical geometry is outside this reviewed method. Nonzero shared density/geoid errors require an explicitly supplied interstation covariance instead of guessed station independence.

This seam was disclosed to main before implementation through the coordinating conversation. It adds a metric derivative boundary, not new keys inside the existing station-correction contract. Full field, worker, online-resource and web/browser gates remain open and main-owned.
