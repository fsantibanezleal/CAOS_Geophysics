# M06 measured MT sounding: feature requirements

Date: 2026-09-28. Parent: [product SDD](../../SDD.md), M05 and M06. This is a bounded offline scientific vertical. The approved product SDD permits feature implementation; no web, API, publication, or deployment change is in scope here. The upstream station label is a screening observation, never automatic permission to run an inverse.

| ID | EARS requirement | Named failing gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MT-0601 | WHEN a measured EDI candidate is registered, THE source ledger SHALL identify its exact provider object, retrieval size and source digest, attribution, licence, and ignored raw location. | `tests/data/test_sources.py::test_auslamp_c15_source_contract` and `tests/numerics/test_mt_field_qc.py::test_pinned_field_receipt` |
| MT-0602 | WHEN the candidate is screened, THE QC runner SHALL compare every original frequency, impedance and variance block with the community EDI parser and reject missing, nonfinite, nonpositive or silently replaced values. | `tests/numerics/test_mt_field_qc.py::test_raw_blocks_and_parser_agree` and `::test_bad_variance_and_rotation_fail_closed` |
| MT-0603 | WHEN the tensor frame is assessed, THE QC runner SHALL verify declared channel orientation and electric-dipole endpoint polarity, orthogonality, the common declared Z rotation, and the absence of a covariance-free arbitrary rerotation. | `tests/numerics/test_mt_field_qc.py::test_geometry_and_rotation_invariance` |
| MT-0604 | WHEN dimensionality is assessed, THE QC runner SHALL report a phase-tensor diagnostic separately from the full complex 1D tensor consistency test, using every supplied period and a conservative variance interpretation fixed before candidate evaluation. | `tests/numerics/test_mt_field_qc.py::test_independent_tensor_gate` |
| MT-0605 | IF any source, frame, error, or complex 1D consistency gate fails, THEN THE M06 runner SHALL emit an explicit ineligible verdict with no fitted model, predicted field response, held-out fit, or field geological truth. | `tests/numerics/test_mt_field_qc.py::test_ineligible_never_becomes_inverse` |
| MT-0606 | WHERE a future measured station clears all source and dimensionality gates, THE M06 workflow SHALL require a separately pinned source and reviewed inversion amendment before fitting; any admitted inverse SHALL use training frequencies only, a training-only halfspace baseline, a frozen held-out partition, multi-start and thickness/beta sensitivity, and uncertainty conditional on its declared error law. | `tests/numerics/test_mt_field_qc.py::test_ineligible_never_becomes_inverse` plus a pinned field-run receipt and reviewed feature-SDD amendment; C15 remains ineligible. |

MT-0606 is a conditional admission rule, not an assertion that the field inverse exists. A source that fails MT-0604 cannot be rescued by adjusting errors, picking a favorable frequency window after inspection, or treating the provider's phase-tensor label as a full-impedance verdict.
