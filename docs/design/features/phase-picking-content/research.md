# M08 and M13 source review, 2026-09-28

This note is the source record for the bounded frontend content unit. It does not report a Geophysics checkpoint, field benchmark, or deployed picker.

## Primary-source facts

- [Zhu and Beroza, PhaseNet, GJI 216 (2019), DOI 10.1093/gji/ggy423](https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggy423), with [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1803.03211): the reference method takes three-component traces, represents analyst P and S picks as Gaussian label distributions (0.1 s standard deviation in that study), standardizes each component, resamples to 100 Hz, and predicts sample-wise noise/P/S probabilities. Figure 5 and section 3 describe a 1D U-Net modification with four down/up stages, skip connections, softmax, and cross-entropy. Its illustrated input is 3 by 3001 samples. These are literature specifications, not measured settings of this product.
- The PhaseNet paper divides recordings by stratified sampling based on stations. That statement does **not** establish mutually exclusive event IDs and station IDs across splits. The approved product SDD requires a stricter event-and-station-disjoint test. We must not quote paper performance as our own or as evidence that our stricter split passed.
- [AI4EPS official PhaseNet repository](https://github.com/AI4EPS/PhaseNet) documents MiniSEED/SAC/HDF5/NumPy prediction, 100 Hz as the default sample rate, phase-score thresholds and optional StationXML for amplitude. It is an implementation reference, not permission to redistribute an unreviewed checkpoint or field trace, and not evidence that browser export works here.
- [Allen, BSSA 68 (1978), DOI 10.1785/bssa0680051521](https://doi.org/10.1785/bssa0680051521) and [ObsPy's `classic_sta_lta`](https://docs.obspy.org/packages/autogen/obspy.signal.trigger.classic_sta_lta.html) establish the classical energy-ratio onset detector. The [ObsPy source](https://docs.obspy.org/_modules/obspy/signal/trigger.html) squares the trace, averages over short and long windows in samples, and applies a thresholded onset function. A trigger alone does not infer a P or S label. [Diehl et al., BSSA 99 (2009), DOI 10.1785/0120080019](https://doi.org/10.1785/0120080019) and their [primary reproducibility supplement](https://www.seismosoc.org/Publications/BSSA_html/bssa_99-3/2008019-esupp/) describe an S workflow with horizontal STA/LTA, polarization, AR-AIC refinement and quality checks. Those choices illustrate why S association is separate; their Alpine-region parameters are not portable M08 settings. A held-out analyst label cannot be used as the test picker input.
- [ObsPy `Inventory.get_response`](https://docs.obspy.org/packages/autogen/obspy.core.inventory.inventory.Inventory.get_response.html) selects the channel response at a specified time; its example distinguishes ground velocity in m/s from digital counts. [ObsPy `remove_response`](https://docs.obspy.org/packages/autogen/obspy.core.stream.Stream.remove_response.html) performs deconvolution in place, so the raw MiniSEED object must remain separately immutable. [FDSN StationXML](https://docs.fdsn.org/projects/stationxml/en/latest/) is the instrument/channel metadata standard.
- The approved [product SDD](../../SDD.md) sets M08 waveform/response QC and M13 training, real held-out evaluation, same-trace M08 comparison and browser parity as **future acceptance gates**. It does not assert they are complete. The [SCEDC archive](https://scedc.caltech.edu/data/cloud.html) is a candidate source, with retrieval, rights, hashes and matching event/station metadata still to be established.

## Design consequences and non-claims

The content may explain a reproducible processing and comparison protocol, but must label parameter values from the original paper as reference values, not frozen local settings. It must carry both digital counts and response-corrected physical units, sampling rate in Hz, time in seconds and UTC, missing-channel/gap handling, and the requirement that training and inference transformations match. A normalized network input is dimensionless, not a new measured ground-motion quantity.

The proposed evaluation partitions by both event and station identity before fitting normalization or selecting thresholds. A recording that bridges separately assigned event and station partitions is excluded and counted, never duplicated. Both pickers see the same held-out traces, windows, quality flags and analyst picks; method-specific transforms are recorded. Matching tolerance, thresholds, SNR strata and abstention rules must be fixed on training/validation data. A softmax score is not a calibrated confidence interval; analyst pick scatter and empirical calibration are distinct evidence. No phase picker here is an acoustic FWI solver.
