## Correction states and physical quantities

Primary [USGS operational report, OFR 02-98 appendix](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/0098/APPENDIX.HTM) records timed base measurements, compensation, lag, heading, altitude difficulties, gridding and reference subtraction as distinct operations. [USGS marine magnetic metadata](https://cmgds.marine.usgs.gov/catalog/pcmsc/DataReleases/ScienceBase/DR_F7GT5K8R/B-04-12-NC_Magnetic_metadata.html) documents directional microlevel filtering and its parameters. These are examples of processing semantics, not Charleston acquisition settings.

| Operation | Physical meaning and evidence prerequisite |
| --- | --- |
| Calibration/compensation | Instrument/platform response, not Earth field; retain provider tests and sensor identities. No scalar compensation coefficients inferred from crossover residuals. |
| Lag | Time alignment of sensor and navigation, including sensor lever arm; needs genuine ordered times/positions and signed delay definition. No displacement from guessed aircraft speed. |
| Diurnal | Time-varying external/background field relative to a named base/reference; needs synchronized overlap and station/reference definition. IGRF secular variation is not diurnal correction. |
| Heading | Repeatable orientation/platform bias; needs heading calibration/coefficients and azimuth convention, not arbitrary geological flattening. |
| IGRF/reference | Main-field model at specified generation, epoch/date, latitude/longitude, altitude convention and vector basis. Existing residual cannot have its reference subtracted again. |
| Tie leveling | Constrained mismatch adjustment at comparable line/tie crossings; height separation can create real physical differences. Fix gauges/components and preserve crossovers that cannot be compared. |
| Microlevel | Further directional residual stripe removal, which can also remove real line-parallel geological signal. Not an independent measured correction or a synonym for tie leveling. |

Raw total intensity F, provider residual Delta T, individual B components, gradiometry in nT/m, magnetic potential in nT-km, and display RGB are separate quantities. Intake can inspect/correct scalar total intensity, but the harmonic fit accepts ONLY one declared scalar anomaly channel in nT with documented reference removal or an explicitly authored/justified constant-background weak projection. A variable-vector field magnitude is not automatically a harmonic scalar. Vector and gradient joint processing require a different reviewed contract, not column relabeling. Providers' applied states remain evidence-backed declarations; unknown states block additional physical corrections.

## IGRF and orientation evidence

Primary [NOAA IGRF page](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/international-geomagnetic-reference-field), [official IGRF14 Fortran](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/IAGA/vmod/igrf14.f) and [coefficient text](https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/IAGA/vmod/coeffs/igrf14coeffs.txt) were retrieved. Their hashes are in the receipt. IGRF14 describes the main field and secular variation, not local geology or storm/diurnal removal. Its nominal interval ends at 2030 even though the official routine permits warned extrapolation to 2035. This design refuses unreviewed extrapolation.

The official source labels X north, Y east, Z down, nT; declination east-positive and inclination down-positive. Product geometry uses east/north/up, so conversion is (Y,X,-Z). Field vectors, F and I/D must be internally consistent. The Fortran geodetic interface labels height in km above sea level while its implementation uses a WGS84 spheroid: this is an explicit altitude-semantics review point, not authority to silently substitute ellipsoidal or orthometric height. A submitted official-evaluation receipt must name the exact height convention and any transformation. This packet does not compile/evaluate IGRF or establish Charleston's original reference epoch.

An anomaly re-reference is add old reference then subtract new reference, only with the old generation, epoch, geometry and base level recoverable. Merely applying the current model to an already corrected anomaly is invalid. Constant study field directions are authored assumptions, not actual IGRF site values.

## Engines, interpolation, continuation and remanence

Verified official [Harmonica 0.7.0 EquivalentSources](https://www.fatiando.org/harmonica/v0.7.0/api/generated/harmonica.EquivalentSources.html) uses inverse-distance harmonic kernels in metric Cartesian coordinates. Its [tagged source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fatiando/harmonica/v0.7.0/harmonica/_equivalent_sources/cartesian.py) calls Verde least_squares. [Verde 1.9.0 implementation](https://www.fatiando.org/verde/v1.9.0/_modules/verde/base/least_squares.html) scales columns with population standard deviation without mean removal and passes sample weights to Ridge(alpha=damping, fit_intercept=False). A normalized objective silently chosen instead would change damping meaning.

Equivalent-source coefficients describe a harmonic representation, not density, susceptibility, magnetization or geological depth. Sources/weights must be built from training data only. Source block aggregation is a separately recorded basis reduction; all eligible observations remain in the fit. It is not permission to thin measurements silently. Explicit sources and single-thread float64 execution avoid hidden library defaults.

The [SciPy 1.15.2 linear interpolator](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.15.2/reference/generated/scipy.interpolate.LinearNDInterpolator.html) provides a barycentric/Delaunay geometric comparator with no physical altitude transfer. It must be named interpolation, and restricted to a comparable-height scalar product. [Harmonica upward continuation](https://www.fatiando.org/harmonica/v0.7.0/api/generated/harmonica.upward_continuation.html) operates on regular northing/easting grids; FFT assumptions include level surface, complete rectangle and boundary extension. Variable-height scalar observations can instead be fit by harmonic equivalent sources and predicted on an explicit higher plane; this has assumptions and cannot manufacture unmeasured high-frequency content.

[Harmonica RTP](https://www.fatiando.org/harmonica/v0.7.0/api/generated/harmonica.reduction_to_pole.html) requires regional field and total magnetization direction. Its default magnetization direction assumes no remanence/demagnetization. EquivalentSources itself cannot do RTP. M03 therefore refuses RTP in its first executable contract; it preserves the requested ineligible reason and M04 handoff. Unknown remanence cannot become induced-only permission. The [dipole engine](https://www.fatiando.org/harmonica/v0.7.0/api/generated/harmonica.dipole_magnetic.html) returns nT for moments in A m^2, enabling a separate analytic-vector oracle. The [total-field helper](https://www.fatiando.org/harmonica/v0.7.0/api/generated/harmonica.total_field_anomaly.html) implements the projected weak-anomaly convention, not the exact nonlinear magnitude difference.

## Sampling and validation

The smallest grid pixel is not the survey's resolved wavelength. Along-track gaps, cross-line spacing, height variation, tie density and signal bandwidth determine what is identifiable. Uniform samples with interval s have nominal Nyquist wavelength 2s; irregular acquisition needs local spacing distributions and declared support masks, not one global Nyquist label. A 200 m rendered grid from 800 m lines does not resolve 400 m cross-line geology.

[Verde BlockKFold](https://www.fatiando.org/verde/v1.9.0/api/generated/verde.BlockKFold.html) addresses spatial correlation, but its groups argument is ignored: it cannot by itself keep flight IDs disjoint. M03 explicitly constructs sealed full-line spatial partitions and uses Verde only inside an already eligible training partition. Flight/tie crossover information, provider corrections and source basis selection can leak test values; blocking random points after whole-survey leveling is insufficient. Acquisition-corrected provider channels whose corrections used the full survey are labelled conditional provider-product prediction, never independently sealed end-to-end preprocessing.

Power spectra show acquisition striping and aliasing only under documented sampling/window/normalization. No depth-from-spectrum claim is promised. A withheld-line residual measures conditional prediction, not geology uniqueness or calibrated field error. Unknown errors stay unknown; uniform numerical weights are a declared fitting choice and never a synthetic field uncertainty.
