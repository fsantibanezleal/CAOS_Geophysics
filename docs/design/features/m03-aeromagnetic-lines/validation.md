## Synthetic acceptance, without field substitution

Each regime is separate and at least one reverse/heterogeneous line is retained in every usable partition. Fit parameters are fixed before opening outer values. S1 smooth analytic field compares heldout predictions at actual heterogeneous heights: proposed RMSE <=5% of heldout signal RMS, with a 1e-6 nT absolute floor for zero controls. If it fails, revise the method/plan transparently before approval, do not relax it after tuning on test data. S2 exact leveling and S3 correction checks use their independent known calibration data. Actual generator/partition/engine and independent numerical gates remain prospective. Do not claim the5% S1 science target from that geometry check. Source/kernel fallback refusal and inverse-variance unit/oracle cases are mandatory even when the nominal control uses unweighted fitting.
