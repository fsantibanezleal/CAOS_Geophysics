# M07 Slagdump ERT requirements

Date: 2026-09-28. Parent: [approved product SDD](../../SDD.md), M07 and sections 2, 5, 6. Scope is the offline, provider-linked Slagdump field profile only; no API, frontend, host or public raw-data change. The observed file is a 5,435-byte, SHA-256-pinned `.ohm` with 38 electrodes and 222 resistances, no supplied `k`, `err`, current, voltage or reciprocal pairs. The fixed upstream example-data tree has no licence file, and the file credits BGR Hannover and Markus Furche. Raw redistribution therefore remains unapproved.

| ID | EARS requirement | Named gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ERT-01 | WHEN Slagdump is selected, THE local workflow SHALL require the existing ledger's exact raw hash/byte and ignored acquisition receipt, without rewriting the raw file or publishing its measurements. | `tests/data/test_ert_data.py::test_pinned_source_and_ignored_receipt` and `git ls-files data/downloads data/raw` |
| ERT-02 | WHEN a `.ohm` file is parsed, THE adapter SHALL require the declared sensor/data counts, `#x z` and `#a b m n R` columns, finite metre positions, finite nonzero Ohm resistances, 1-based valid distinct ABMN indices, unique quadrupoles, equally spaced Wenner/reciprocal index geometry and no trailing undeclared payload; malformed input SHALL fail with a named field or row. | `tests/data/test_ert_data.py::test_strict_sensor_and_quadrupole_contract` |
| ERT-03 | WHEN QC runs, THE workflow SHALL preserve every accepted reading, report topographic span, AB spacing, resistance sign/range, duplicate/reciprocal availability and flag-only outliers. IF no reciprocal pairs or instrument errors exist, THEN it SHALL not invent reciprocal error estimates or calibrated uncertainty. | `tests/data/test_ert_data.py::test_qc_and_absent_reciprocals` |
| ERT-04 | WHEN geometric factors are computed, THE workflow SHALL calculate the independent flat-halfspace four-electrode formula and pyGIMLi's numerical topography-aware factor with signed convention. THE flat analytical factor SHALL agree with pyGIMLi's flat factor to relative `1e-5`, and a flat homogeneous forward control SHALL recover its specified resistivity to within 5% median relative error. | `tests/numerics/test_ert.py::test_topographic_factor_and_inverse` and `::test_flat_homogeneous_oracle` |
| ERT-05 | WHILE source errors are absent, WHEN field inversion is attempted, THE workflow SHALL label `max(0.03*abs(R), 0.001 Ohm)` as an assumed local weighting floor, state that chi-square is conditional on it, use a fixed predeclared topographic 2D mesh/regularization, and keep source/mesh/engine/parameter hashes in an ignored reproducible receipt. | `tests/numerics/test_ert.py::test_field_result_receipt_and_uncertainty` |
| ERT-06 | WHEN a field inverse is run, THE workflow SHALL withhold a deterministic spacing-stratified approximately 20% of quadrupoles *and*, as a stricter independent check, the central 40-60% survey-midpoint block in a separate fit. It SHALL retain signed observed/predicted/residual arrays and sensitivity/coverage, and compare each held-out log-resistance RMSE against its own training-only homogeneous baseline. It SHALL claim a predictive inverse only if finite positive model/predictions, normal engine stops, and at least 10% improvement on **both** splits are observed without tuning on either holdout. | `tests/numerics/test_ert.py::test_field_holdout_and_inverse_gate` |
| ERT-07 | IF forward/oracle, held-out, or inverse gates fail, THEN THE workflow SHALL retain QC and the failed diagnostic receipt but label M07 `not-converged`, `ineligible` or `unverified` as appropriate, never a successful field inversion or known geology. | `tests/numerics/test_ert.py::test_failed_inverse_never_claims_success` |
| ERT-08 | THE source guide and feature convergence SHALL document provider/BGR provenance and unresolved redistribution rights, source and unit assumptions, topographic factor equation, split, error surrogate, model/residual/sensitivity interpretation, local reproduction and all unmet gates. | `tests/data/test_ert_data.py::test_m07_documented_boundaries` and `docs/design/features/m07-slagdump-ert/convergence.md` |

Numerical thresholds are declared before the blocked-split implementation. A flat homogeneous control checks sign and scale; it cannot by itself validate the non-flat inverse. Both tests predict withheld readings on one line, not independent geology, transfer to another site, or a calibrated posterior. The central block is added as an explicit anti-leakage challenge because neighboring Wenner quadrupoles share electrodes.
