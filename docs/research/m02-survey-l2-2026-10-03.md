P-L2-02. [Pinned misfit source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simpeg/simpeg/v0.25.2/simpeg/data_misfit.py)
is VERIFIED against installed exact bytes. The objective is unhalved weighted
squared residual, the gradient applies W.T W, and the Hessian-vector implementation
applies W W. Therefore an arbitrary lower-triangular Cholesky inverse is NOT
compatible with this Hessian. The proposed full-covariance adapter instead uses
a symmetric inverse square root and a legacy sparse matrix for multiplication.
No patch to vendor source or a custom replacement misfit is authorized.

P-L2-03. [SciPy 1.15.2 eigh](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.15.2/reference/generated/scipy.linalg.eigh.html)
and [solve_triangular](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy-1.15.2/reference/generated/scipy.linalg.solve_triangular.html)
are VERIFIED API documents. For declared real SPD C, the proposed derivation is
C=U Lambda U.T, W=U Lambda^(-1/2) U.T. Independently compare r.T solve(C,r)
and Cholesky triangular-solve squared norm; eigensolver output alone does not
validate symmetry, positivity, units or row alignment. Eigh does not check input
symmetry. Exact covariance symmetry and a declared conditioning bound are separate
admission rules, not silently added diagonal noise or clipping of eigenvalues.

## Proposed mathematical design derived from that evidence

For a fixed fit subset f, q=rho/1000, r=G_f q+b_f-d_f, let
phi_d=||W_f r||^2, phi_m=||R(q-q_ref)||^2. Choose candidates in the
mean-data objective phi_d/n_f + beta_candidate phi_m, executed by official
unhalved SimPEG with beta_engine=n_f beta_candidate. This stated scaling makes
the calibration parameter comparable across differently sized folds; it is not
a discrepancy-driven beta update or a hidden adjustment against holdout results.

Then gradient=2G_f.T W_f.T W_f r + 2 beta_engine R.T R(q-q_ref),
H v=2G_f.T W_f.T W_f G_f v + 2 beta_engine R.T R v. The physical-density
gradient and Hessian-vector chain require factors 1/1000 and 1/1000000.
The prediction residual exported to users is d-p; optimizer residual is p-d.
No default demeaning, background fitting, absolute-gravity corrections or error
floor is inferred. Positive smallness gives a unique regularized finite-grid
optimum under numeric conditions; it does NOT make density identified by gravity.

Geometry-only partition planning must occur before values are delivered.
Calibration receives development observations only; sealed outer observations
enter a separate evaluation request after candidate selection/model freeze.
Use marginal principal covariance blocks, never Schur conditioning on outer
values or whitening a combined fit/holdout vector. Correlation across blocks
remains disclosed and the test remains conditional on declared errors/geometry.
Learned training is not-applicable; beta selection is explicit calibration.
