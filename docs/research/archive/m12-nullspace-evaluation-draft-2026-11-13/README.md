# Archived M12 null-space evaluation draft

Status: archived research draft, not a declared product manuscript and not a proposed-method novelty claim.

The complete LaTeX source, generated PDF, table builder, and coverage metadata are preserved here. The draft evaluates a post-hoc null-space projection on a bounded synthetic protocol. Null-space projection and related learned null-space methods predate this study in travel-time tomography, seismic inversion, and reflectivity inversion. The study does not establish a distinct algorithmic contribution, a same-data comparison with modern methods, or field validity.

The draft also uses the earlier residual-v3.1 experiment, so its reported values are not the current physics-v2 BS-1 result. Do not use it as a release manuscript until its protocol, checkpoint provenance, comparisons, and scientific contribution have been reassessed.

Primary-source review: [M12 travel-time literature review](../../m12-learned-traveltime-review-2026-10-30.md). The current proposed-method paper is in `manuscripts/geometry-normalized-traveltime-refinement-v001/`.
